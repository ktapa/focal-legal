Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Focal is a personal planning application. This policy explains the information Focal stores and how its optional Google Calendar connection works.

Information Focal stores

Focal stores the account identity needed to sign you in, including your email address, display name when provided, and authentication identity. It also stores the planning information you enter, including tasks, projects, milestones, time blocks, notes, rituals, and account or planning settings.

This information is used to authenticate you and provide your private Focal workspace and its planning features.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Focal requests read-only access limited to events on calendars you own. When Focal displays those events, it fetches them from Google on demand. Focal never writes changes back to Google Calendar, and Google event data is never persisted in Focal's database.

The Google refresh token used for the connection is held as a secret in Supabase Vault. It is not stored in Focal's browser storage or in a plaintext database column.

Disconnecting Google Calendar deletes Focal's stored credential and connection record locally. Focal then directs you to Google's third-party access page, where you can complete removal of Focal's access on Google's side. Focal does not revoke the Google grant automatically because the same Google authorization may also be used for sign-in.
